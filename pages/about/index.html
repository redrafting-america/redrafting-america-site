About

Meet the organization, people, standards, and public commitments responsible for carrying Redrafting America from an ambitious idea to a credible civic institution.

What you’ll find here

Our Story

The project’s origins, evolution, and reason for existing. Why it matters: constitutional reform should never appear without context; readers deserve to understand what prompted the work and how its purpose developed.

Mission

The organization’s governing purpose, core principles, and long-term civic objective. Why it matters: a clear mission establishes what the organization is authorized to pursue—and what lies outside its mandate. Read the mission.

Leadership

Current officers, directors, their responsibilities, and open positions. Why it matters: the public should be able to identify who exercises authority, what each leader is accountable for, and where the organization still needs qualified service.

Contributors & Advisors

People who materially contribute expertise, review, research, or stewardship without holding formal governing authority. Why it matters: meaningful contributions deserve recognition, and readers should be able to distinguish advice from official decision-making.

Governance

Bylaws, organizational authority, board responsibilities, and approved internal policies. Why it matters: a project devoted to stronger public institutions must demonstrate disciplined governance within its own institution. Review the pending Bylaws Table of Contents.

Transparency & Accountability

Organizational status, public reporting, funding disclosures, conflicts, major decisions, and standards of accountability. Why it matters: public trust must be earned through verifiable conduct, not requested on the strength of good intentions.

Project Roadmap

Major milestones from prelaunch through constitutional publication, education, public review, and the future Convention. Why it matters: a visible roadmap lets readers judge progress honestly and separates current work from future aspirations.
