How We're Organized

Eleven Pillars, Each With a Leader and a Department

Every pillar represents something the organization has to protect to remain trustworthy over time — vision, continuity, history, resources, law, community, knowledge, sustainability, integrity, innovation, and execution. Each pillar is led by a Director (or, for the four officer seats required under our Bylaws, by the President, Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer), who in turn oversees the specific functions listed under their department.

Tier 1What We Protect Tier 2Who Leads Each Pillar Tier 3How We Execute the Mission

The organizational chart is arranged in eleven colored columns and three horizontal tiers. Tier one names what each pillar protects: Vision, Continuity, History, Resources, Law, Community, Knowledge, Sustainability, Integrity, Innovation, and Execution. Tier two names the accountable officer or director. Tier three lists the department functions that execute each pillar's mission.
