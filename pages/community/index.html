Town Hall

Town Hall is the working label for the future Digital Constitutional Convention: a structured public environment for citizen deliberation, evidence, moderation, proposals, and voting. No Town Hall page or participation system is currently available. Why it matters: constitutional participation needs safeguards and procedures designed for thoughtful public judgment rather than the engagement incentives of ordinary social media.

Official Social Media

This page identifies Redrafting America’s official social platforms and uses @redraftingamerica as the standard public handle where each service permits it. Each platform entry explains its intended use and clearly marks accounts that have not yet been created, allowing visitors to distinguish active official channels from future ones or possible impersonation. Why it matters: timely public communication is only useful when readers can verify that it is authentic.

Available now

The Drafting Room

The Drafting Room is the project’s active publication library for developed Papers and more exploratory Drafting Notes. It currently provides five searchable publications, with summaries, categories, topic tags, dates, reading times, and permanent article pages covering the project’s origins, institutional resilience, constitutional design, civic warnings, and public participation. Why it matters: readers can examine the arguments and evolving thinking behind the formal constitutional work in a more personal editorial voice.
