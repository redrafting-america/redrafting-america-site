Town Hall

The future Digital Constitutional Convention and its structured tools for citizen deliberation, evidence, moderation, and voting. Why it matters: constitutional participation needs a process designed for thoughtful public judgment rather than the incentives of ordinary social media.

Social Media

Verified official channels for timely announcements and informal public conversation. Why it matters: readers should be able to distinguish authentic project communications from impersonation or unofficial commentary. Find our official social channels.

The Drafting Room

Papers explaining the reasoning, concerns, history, and ideas behind the work in a voice more personal than formal constitutional text. Why it matters: readers deserve to see the thinking that shapes the project and how that thinking develops. Enter The Drafting Room.

Events

Public programs, civic gatherings, educational briefings, and future Convention milestones. Why it matters: a national civic project must create opportunities for people to learn and participate beyond solitary website reading.

Partner Directory

Organizations and institutions participating in education, research, outreach, or lawful adoption work. Why it matters: transparent partnerships show how the project’s reach is built and which institutions share responsibility for particular efforts.
