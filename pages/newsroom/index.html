What you’ll find here

Content outline

Board Minutes

The current outline defines the format for approved public minutes and related Board records. Planned entries will identify the meeting date and type, attendance, motions and votes, approved actions, any lawful public redactions, and the document-integrity information needed to verify the record. No meeting minutes are currently listed. Why it matters: publishing the governing record allows the public to see how organizational authority is exercised over time.

Content outline

Press Releases

The current outline defines a permanent archive for official organizational announcements. Each future release is expected to carry a publication date, contact information, a downloadable copy, visible correction notices when needed, and links to the underlying publication, project page, or Board record. No press releases are currently listed. Why it matters: an authoritative archive should show exactly what the organization announced and when.

Content outline

News Coverage

The current outline defines a chronological record of independent reporting, interviews, and substantive external discussion about Redrafting America. It calls for publication, headline, author, date, and source information; a clear separation between independent coverage and organizational writing; and limited project responses when a material factual correction is necessary. No coverage entries are currently listed. Why it matters: outside scrutiny helps readers evaluate the project beyond its own account of itself.

Content outline

Media Kit & Inquiries

The current outline defines the information and materials intended for working journalists: a designated inquiry process, approved short and long descriptions of the organization, current leadership biographies and titles, authorized logos and brand guidance, and a factual reference sheet. The complete media kit has not yet been published. Why it matters: accurate reporting is easier when journalists can find reliable source material and the appropriate organizational contact in one place.

Available now

Cultural Influences

Cultural Influences is an active, curated collection of music, film, and television connected to the project’s themes of truth, accountability, democratic responsibility, and human dignity. Visitors can filter the collection, open source material, and read an editorial summary, a selected quotation when available, and a specific explanation of why each work matters to Redrafting America. Why it matters: documenting these influences makes the project’s intellectual and cultural lineage visible rather than leaving it implicit.
