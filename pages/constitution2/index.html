Constitution v2.0

Explore the proposed constitutional framework, the reasoning behind every provision, and the public record showing how the text changes over time.

What you’ll find here

Declaration of Reformation

The formal case for undertaking peaceful constitutional reform and the conditions that make that work necessary. Why it matters: before proposing a replacement framework, the project must explain why reform is justified and what lawful purpose it serves.

Preamble

The opening statement of popular authority, shared purpose, and the ends the Constitution is designed to serve. Why it matters: the Preamble supplies the moral and civic orientation against which the document’s institutions should be understood.

Articles

The official constitutional text, published in a stable numbered sequence with sections and clauses that can be cited precisely. Why it matters: the proposal must be evaluated as real governing language, not merely as a collection of policy preferences.

Explainers

A paired guide for every Article explaining what it means, why that direction was chosen, the alternatives considered, and the supporting sources. Why it matters: constitutional language should be understandable and its design choices should be open to scrutiny.

Comparison

A structured comparison showing what Constitution v2.0 preserves, changes, adds, or removes from the existing Constitution. Why it matters: readers need a direct way to evaluate the proposal against the system it is intended to strengthen or replace.

Version History

A dated public record of every material constitutional revision, the reason for it, and the authority that approved it. Why it matters: constitutional drafting must be traceable, reviewable, and protected from silent alteration.
