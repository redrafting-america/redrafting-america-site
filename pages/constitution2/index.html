Constitution v2.0

Explore the proposed constitutional framework, the reasoning behind every provision, and the public record showing how the text changes over time.

What you’ll find here

Content outline

Declaration of Reformation

The current outline defines the Declaration as the public case for considering peaceful constitutional reform. Its planned sections cover the purpose of the Declaration, the categories of institutional concern motivating the project, the standard that should be met before proposing constitutional change, and the Declaration’s relationship to the binding Articles. Why it matters: the project should explain why reform is justified and what lawful purpose it serves before asking anyone to evaluate a replacement framework.

Content outline

Preamble

The current outline defines the future Preamble page as the home of the approved opening text, a plain explanation of its commitments, guidance about its intended role and limits in constitutional interpretation, and a dated revision record. The constitutional Preamble itself has not yet been released on this page. Why it matters: readers will need to distinguish the Preamble’s statement of popular authority and shared purpose from the operative rules contained in the Articles.

Content outline

Articles

The current outline defines the official Table of Contents for Constitution v2.0. It calls for every Article to appear in constitutional order with a clear publication status, a stable permanent page for released text, a direct connection to its paired Explainer, and a revision history. No constitutional Articles are presently published in this section. Why it matters: constitutional proposals must be available as stable, numbered governing language that can be cited, compared, and reviewed precisely.

Content outline

Article Explainers

The current outline defines a companion record for each constitutional Article. Every Explainer is intended to describe what the Article does, why its direction was chosen, the legal, historical, policy, and institutional evidence supporting it, the material alternatives and tradeoffs considered, and a direct cross-link to the official text. Why it matters: readers should be able to examine both the proposed rule and the reasoning behind it without needing specialized legal training.

Content outline

Constitution Comparison

The current outline defines a provision-by-provision comparison between Constitution v2.0 and the existing Constitution. It will identify what the proposal preserves, changes, adds, or removes and connect those differences to the relevant Articles and explanations. The comparison itself has not yet been populated. Why it matters: readers need a direct account of how the proposed system differs from the one it is intended to strengthen or replace.

Content outline

Version History

The current outline defines the authoritative revision record for the proposed Constitution. It is designed to identify the current version and publication date, list material changes chronologically, record the authority and date associated with each revision, retain stable references to superseded text, and distinguish corrections from substantive amendments. Why it matters: constitutional drafting must be traceable and protected from silent alteration.
