The Four Design Principles of Constitution v2.0

Unify Services

Consolidate related governmental functions into coherent constitutional institutions.

Separate Powers

Assign distinct constitutional responsibilities to prevent concentrations of power.

Eliminate Duplication

Reduce overlapping agencies and simplify government through clear institutional design.

Preserve Accountability

Ensure every governmental function has a clearly identifiable constitutional steward.

Constitutional Inspirations

Roman Republic

Rome kept its public treasury, the Aerarium, in the Temple of Saturn. Our Aerarium carries its name and its duty of civic stewardship.

Latin Civic Tradition

Each branch takes its name from a Latin virtue: concordia, diligentia, aequitas, sapientia, veritas. The name tells you what the branch must honor.

Sun Yat-sen’s Five-Powers Constitution

Sun Yat-sen added examination and oversight to the classic three powers. Constitution v2.0 gives each its own branch: the Sapientium and the Veritium.

German Eternity Clauses

After watching democracy get destroyed from within, postwar Germany placed human dignity beyond amendment. So do our Twelve Immutable Laws.
